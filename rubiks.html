How to read it

R
Right face
L
Left face
U
Up (top) face
D
Down (bottom) face
F
Front face (facing you)
B
Back face

A letter alone = turn that face clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it.
' (say "prime") = counter-clockwise. R' undoes R.
2 = half turn. R2 is the same as R R.
