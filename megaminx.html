The Megaminx is a dodecahedron: 12 faces, each a pentagon, with 50 pieces that move. It's solved a lot like a 3x3, just with more of everything. Learn the moves, then follow the beginner method below. Time your solves in the ⏱ Timer (choose Megaminx as the event).

How to read it

Hold it with white on top and green at the front. The top face is U, and the five faces around it are:

FFront (facing you)
RRight
LLeft
BRBack-right
BLBack-left

The lower faces are FR and FL (below the front corners), DR, DL, B and D (the bottom).

A letter alone = turn that face a fifth of a turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it. ' ("prime") = counter-clockwise. 2 = two-fifths, so U2 is the same as U U.

Scrambles (the dashed buttons)

Official scrambles use big moves instead:

  • R++ / R--: hold the left part still and turn everything else two-fifths of a turn.
  • D++ / D--: hold the top face still and turn everything under it two-fifths of a turn.
  • U / U': turn just the top face, as usual.

The plan

If you can solve a 3x3 with the beginner method, you already know most of this! You build the Megaminx one ring at a time, from one face down to the opposite face.

  1. Star: the 5 edges around your first face.
  2. First-face corners with the corner trick.
  3. The rings in the middle, by feel, with the edge tricks and the corner trick.
  4. Last face: 4 small steps, with the Flip, the Sune (or Gap Sune), the Niklas and the corner trick again.

In the moving pictures the top face is always the one you're working on, so it's white there. On your Megaminx, start with any colour you like.

1The star

Pick a first face and keep it on top. Bring its 5 edges up around the centre so it makes a star. Each edge's other colour must match the side centre next to it.

  • Do it by feel, like the cross on a 3x3. Find an edge, turn its side face to bring it up, and check the colours.
  • If putting an edge in knocks out one you already placed, turn that side back and try from the other way.

2First-face corners

Still with the first face on top: find a corner that belongs on it. Turn the lower faces to bring it right under its spot, so it sits below the top-front-right corner. Then do the corner trick until the corner is in and its colour is on top. It takes 1 to 5 goes.

The corner trick never moves the other top pieces, so you can't break your star.

🔺 Corner trick

FR is the face under the front-right edge. Do it 6 times and everything is back where it started.

3The rings in the middle

Turn the Megaminx over so your first face is on the bottom. Now fill in the next rings, one at a time, going up. This part is done by feel, like F2L on a 3x3. The tricks help:

  • An edge: tilt the Megaminx so a face with nothing solved on it is on top, the gap is just below its front-right (or front-left) edge, and the edge you want is at its top-front. Do the edge trick for that side. It only moves pieces on the top face, plus the gap.
  • A corner: tilt it the same way, with the corner's spot at the top-front-right and the corner just under it, and do the corner trick until it's in.
  • A piece stuck in the wrong spot? Use a trick to pop it out to the top first, then put it in properly.

Keep going until only the last face (the top) is left.

4The last face

Hold the Megaminx with the unsolved face on top from now on. "Turn the Megaminx" below means turn the whole puzzle in your hands, keeping that face on top.

4a. Make a star: flip the edges

Count the top edges whose top colour is not facing up:

  • 4 wrong: turn the Megaminx so the one good edge is at the back-left. Do the flip.
  • 2 wrong, next to each other: hold them at the front and right. Do the flip.
  • 2 wrong, with a gap: hold them at the front and back-right. Do the flip. Star done!
🔃 Flip

4b. Put the edges in place

Turn the top (U) until as many top edges as possible match the side centres. Then:

  • 2 match, next to each other: turn the Megaminx so they're at the front and left. Do the Sune, then turn the top to check. Not done? Do it again.
  • 2 match, with a gap: hold them at the front and back-right. Do the Gap Sune, then turn the top to check. Not done? Do it again.
  • Only 1 matches: do the Sune from anywhere, then look again.
🌞 Sune

The front and left edges stay put.

🌗 Gap Sune

The front and back-right edges stay put.

4c. Put the corners in place

A corner is in the right place if it has the right three colours for its spot, even if it's twisted. The Niklas moves three corners round; the two corners on the left never move.

  • 2 right, next to each other: turn the Megaminx so they're on the left (front-left and back-left). Do the Niklas. Not done? Do it again.
  • 2 right, with a gap: hold them at the back-left and front-right. Do the Niklas and look again.
  • Just 1 right: hold it at the front-left. Do the Niklas and look again.
  • None right: do the Niklas from anywhere and look again.
🔁 Niklas

4d. Twist the corners

Hold a twisted corner at the top-front-right. Do the corner trick twice. Still twisted? Twice more. The middle of the puzzle looks messed up while you do this. Don't panic, it comes back!

Now turn only the top (U) to bring the next twisted corner to the front-right, and do the same. Never turn the whole Megaminx in this step. When every corner is done, turn the top to line it up. Solved! 🎉
