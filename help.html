🚀 Getting started

New here? This order works well:

  1. Learn the notation. Go to ⏱ Timer → 🔤 Notation and play Name that move! until you can read moves like R U R' U'.
  2. Time some solves. Use the ⏱ Timer tab. Your times are saved automatically.
  3. Learn 2-Look. In the 📚 Alg Trainer, start with the 🌱 2-Look tab: just 16 algorithms to finish the last layer.
  4. Then PLL, then OLL. Learn the 21 PLLs first (they save the most time), then the 57 OLLs.
  5. Check your progress. 🏆 Trophies shows your level, your achievements and how you rank against the world.

Just want to have fun? Try the 🤖 Solver: scramble your cube and let the computer solve it.

Everything is saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no account.

⏱ Timer & Notation

Timing a solve

  1. Pick your puzzle in Event (3x3, 2x2, 4x4, Pyraminx or Skewb).
  2. Scramble your cube with the moves shown. The picture shows what it should look like. Show 3D switches to a 3D view.
  3. Press Space once (or tap the black timer) to start the 15-second inspection. Look at your cube and plan your cross.
  4. Hold Space (or keep your finger on the timer). The time turns red, then green. Let go when it's green and the timer starts.
  5. Press any key (or tap anywhere) to stop.
  • Don't want inspection? Untick 15s inspection. Then just hold and let go.
  • Inspection over 15 seconds adds +2 seconds, and over 17 seconds is a DNF, like at a competition. Esc cancels inspection.
  • Each solve in the list has +2, DNF and ✕ (delete) buttons, in case you messed up.
  • On a computer, point at a solve to see its scramble.

The stats

  • Best: your fastest single solve.
  • ao5: take your last 5 solves, remove the fastest and slowest, and average the other 3. That's how official averages work.
  • ao12: the same with 12 solves.

Notation tab

  • Try the moves: press the buttons and watch the cube turn. Drag the cube to see the back.
  • Name that move!: the cube does one move and you pick which one. Build up a streak! Start on Easy (R, U, F), then try Hard.
  • Algorithm playground: type any algorithm and press Play.

📚 Alg Trainer

  • Tabs: 🌱 2-Look is the beginner set (start here), 🟡 OLL (57) and 🔄 PLL (21) are the full sets, and 🎯 Drill tests you.
  • Tap a card to watch its algorithm on a 3D cube. It also shows moves that set the case up on your real cube: start solved, do those moves, and you're looking at that case.
  • Tap the badge on a card (Not started) to mark it Learning, then tap again for Learned ✓. The bars at the top show your progress.
  • Use Show to list only the cases you're learning, or the ones you haven't started.
  • In the pictures, white is the top colour. It works exactly the same with yellow on top.

The Drill

  1. Choose what to practise. Cases I'm learning is the most useful, but it needs some cards marked Learning first.
  2. Look at the case and work out which one it is. Try it on your real cube if you like.
  3. Press Show answer, then be honest: ✅ I knew it or ❌ Not yet.

Moves in brackets like (U') just turn the top layer to line the case up before or after the algorithm.

📡 Smart Cube

A smart cube has Bluetooth inside, so the computer sees every turn. No smart cube? Use the keyboard cube.

Connecting a smart cube

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone (not iPhone or iPad).
  2. Turn the cube on (give it a twist) and make sure it isn't connected to its own app.
  3. Press Connect GAN cube for GAN, MoYu AI or Monster Go cubes, or Connect other smart cube for GoCube, Rubik's Connected or Giiker.
  4. Pick your cube in the pop-up. The dot turns green when it's connected.
  5. If the cube on screen doesn't match your real cube, solve your real cube and press My cube is solved now.

If a GAN cube asks for a "MAC address", open the GAN app: it's shown in the cube's info, like AB:12:CD:34:EF:56.

Doing a solve

  1. Do the scramble. Moves turn green as you do them. If you turn the wrong way, it says "Oops! Undo with: …". Do those moves and carry on.
  2. When it says Scrambled!, the timer starts on your first turn. No buttons needed.
  3. It stops by itself when the cube is solved.

Scrambled it your own way? Press I scrambled it myself.

What the results mean

  • Cross → F2L → OLL → PLL: how long each step of your solve took. The boxes light up as you finish each step.
  • Moves and TPS (turns per second) for the whole solve and each step.
  • The OLL and PLL cases you got, like "OLL 27 · Sune" or "T-Perm", or OLL skip! / PLL skip! if you were lucky.
  • ▶ Replay solve plays your whole solve back, split into steps. Tap any solve in the History to replay it.

🤖 Solver

Scanning with the camera

  1. Press Turn on camera and allow the camera when asked.
  2. The page tells you which face to show and which colour must be on top. Fit that face inside the grid and press 📸 Capture this face. The order is:
    • Green facing the camera, white on top
    • Red facing the camera, white on top
    • Blue facing the camera, white on top
    • Orange facing the camera, white on top
    • White facing the camera, blue on top
    • Yellow facing the camera, green on top
  3. Messed one up? Redo last face.
  4. Check the cube net under Your cube. If a colour is wrong, tap the right colour in the row of colours, then tap the wrong sticker to fix it.
  5. Press 🤖 Solve it!

Tips for a good scan: use bright, even light (daylight is best), avoid shiny reflections on the stickers, and hold the cube still and square to the camera.

Other ways in

  • 🎨 Paint by hand: pick a colour and tap stickers. Copy your cube with white on top and green facing you.
  • 🎲 Random cube: no cube needed. Watch the computer solve a random scramble.

Following the solution

  • Hold your real cube with white on top and green facing you. This is important!
  • Press Next move ▶ (or the → key) and do each move as it's shown. ◀ Back (or ←) goes back.
  • If it says a corner is twisted or an edge is flipped, your real cube was probably taken apart or twisted by hand. It can't be solved by turning until that's fixed (or a colour was entered wrong).

🏆 Trophies

XP and levels

  • 5 XP for every timed solve (10 XP on the Smart Cube page).
  • 5 XP for each algorithm you're learning, 20 XP for each one you've learned.
  • Achievements give 25 (Common), 75 (Rare), 200 (Epic) or 500 XP (Legendary).
  • Levels get further apart as you go up. Titles go from Cube Rookie all the way to Cube Legend.

Achievements

There are 34. Locked ones are grey and tell you what to do to unlock them. When you unlock one, a pop-up appears in the corner of whatever page you're on.

World ranking

  • Your best single and best average for each puzzle are compared with everyone who has ever competed at an official WCA competition.
  • Try any time lets you check any time for any event, like a goal you're aiming for.
  • Home times are usually a bit faster than competition times: no nerves and no judge!

Looking people up

  • Type a WCA ID (like 2019WANY36) and press Look up to see that cuber's records and personal bests.
  • Don't know someone's ID? Search their name on the WCA website.
  • World Top 10 shows the fastest people for any event. Tap a name to look them up.

📖 Glossary

Algorithm (alg)
A set of moves you learn by heart that does one job, like "swap these two corners".
Notation: R, L, U, D, F, B
Right, Left, Up (top), Down (bottom), Front and Back faces. A letter on its own means turn that face clockwise (as if you're looking at it). ' ("prime") means counter-clockwise. 2 means a half turn.
x, y, z
Turn the whole cube in your hands: x like R, y like U, z like F.
M, r, f…
M is the middle layer (turns like L). A lowercase letter (r) or "w" (Rw) means turn two layers together.
CFOP
The method most speedcubers use: Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL.
Cross
Step 1: make a cross on one face (usually white) with the edges matching the side centres.
F2L (First Two Layers)
Step 2: pair up corners and edges and put them in together, finishing the first two layers.
OLL (Orient the Last Layer)
Step 3: make the whole top face one colour. 57 cases.
PLL (Permute the Last Layer)
Step 4: move the top pieces to the right places, and it's solved! 21 cases.
2-Look OLL / PLL
Doing OLL and PLL in two easier steps each, with only 16 algorithms in total. How most people start.
Skip
When a step is already done by luck, so you skip it. An OLL skip and a PLL skip in the same solve is a "last layer skip". Very rare!
AUF
"Adjust U Face": a U turn before or after an algorithm to line things up.
Headlights
Two corners on one side with the same colour. Used to recognise PLLs.
Sune / Anti-Sune
Nicknames for OLL 27 and 26, two of the most used algorithms.
Scramble
Random moves to mix up the cube. Competitions use computer-made "random-state" scrambles, and so does the Timer.
Inspection
The 15 seconds you get to look at the cube before a solve at a competition.
+2 / DNF
Penalties: +2 adds two seconds; DNF means "Did Not Finish" and the solve doesn't count.
PB
Personal best: your fastest ever time.
ao5 / ao12
Average of 5 / 12: remove the best and worst times, then average the rest.
TPS
Turns per second: how fast your fingers go.
Recognition
Working out which case you have before you do the algorithm. The Drill trains this.
Colour neutral
Being able to start with a cross on any colour, not just white.
WCA / WCA ID
The World Cube Association runs official competitions. Everyone who competes gets a WCA ID, like 2019WANY36.
Smart cube
A cube with Bluetooth inside that tells a computer or phone every move you make.
God's Number
20. Every scrambled 3x3 can be solved in 20 moves or fewer (proved in 2010).
Two-phase algorithm
The clever method computers use to find short solutions. The Solver page explains it.

🛠 Troubleshooting

The 3D cube or scrambles don't show up

These come from the internet (a library called cubing.js). Check you're online and reload the page. The timer still works offline, with simpler scrambles.

Space bar doesn't start the timer

Click an empty part of the page first. If a dropdown or text box is selected, the space bar goes to that instead. Also check you're on the ⏱ Timer tab.

The Bluetooth buttons are greyed out

Your browser can't do Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. iPhones and iPads don't allow Bluetooth in the browser, but you can still use the keyboard cube there.

The smart cube won't connect

  • Twist the cube to wake it up, and close its own app (a cube can only talk to one thing at a time).
  • Make sure Bluetooth is turned on for the computer or phone.
  • Try the other connect button: some GAN cubes work with either one.

The screen cube doesn't match my real cube

A move may have been missed. Solve your real cube, then press My cube is solved now.

The camera won't turn on

  • Allow camera access when the browser asks (look for a camera icon in the address bar if you said no by accident).
  • Cameras only work when the site is opened from an https:// address (like the real website), not from a file on your computer.
  • Close other apps that might be using the camera.

The scan got colours wrong

Fix them on the net: pick the right colour, then tap the wrong sticker. For better scans, use brighter, more even light and avoid glare. Red and orange get mixed up most.

"One corner is twisted" / "One edge is flipped" / "Two pieces are swapped"

First check the colours on the net carefully: one wrong sticker can cause this. If they're right, your real cube was twisted or taken apart by hand and put back wrong. It can't be solved by turning until you fix that piece.

My solves / progress disappeared

Everything is saved in the browser on this device. A different browser, a different device, a private/incognito window, or clearing browsing data will start fresh. Stick to one browser on one device.

World rank or look-up says "Couldn't reach the WCA data"

That part needs the internet. Check you're online and try again. (Your own world rank still works offline.)
